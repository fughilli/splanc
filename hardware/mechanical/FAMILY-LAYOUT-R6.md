# Family layout r6 and editable motion studio

Current CAD input: `output/family-layout-r6/scene.json`, generated by
`output/mechanical-runtime/bin/python hardware/mechanical/revise_family_layout.py`.
The updater preserves the r5 checkpoint and adjusts real STEP solids. Its reference
geometry is r5 and campaign-r3; keep those snapshots when reproducing it.

Splanc and Splanc GNSS share one enclosure. Their two top-entry LED connectors
move to (94,25) and (94,36) mm, at the east edge like Mini. The button row stays
on the south face; USB-C stays at its existing south-left location. The previous
wide front bowl is removed and its roof restored. A narrow east-edge bowl clears
the buttons, flexures and raised seam. Standard and weather concepts are updated.
The interface, authoring input, placement constraints, and both native rough
placements reflect the new connector poses; routing remains unfinished.

MAX shell shrinks from 335×134×53 to 292×134×53 mm (43 mm shorter, excluding
unchanged external power lugs). Pi and HAT rotate together +90 degrees. Ethernet
and USB face north alongside one output bank. Pi power/HDMI face the east end.
The cooler, HAT standoffs and rigid USB bridge follow the same transform; power
board, busbars and LED banks remain fixed. Mounting posts and case end screws are
repositioned. This update covers standard MAX; its weather concept is not promoted
until its seal and thermal design are revisited.

Build the editable Blender project with:
`Blender -b --python hardware/mechanical/build_motion_studio.py`.
Output: `output/blender-motion-studio-r1/splanc-motion-studio.blend`.
Textures and HDRI are packed. Scene 01 is Entry B; scene 02 is Macro Roll;
scene 03 is the updated family asset library. Camera location/lens, camera target,
product transform and highlight light all have separate, sparse editable keys.
Timeline markers identify entry, logo highlight and exit. Read the project's
START HERE text block. Sparse curves are editable approximations of prior clips.

These remain fit prototypes. Nominal button travel/insertion is checked; full
supplier stack-up, spring force/fatigue, injection tooling, sealing, thermal and
USB jumper mating qualification remain open.
