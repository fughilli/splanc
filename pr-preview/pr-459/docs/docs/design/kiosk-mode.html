Kiosk mode¶

A public, locked-down mode for unattended installs (first use: the Halloween 2026 stoop installs, SpookySplanc1/2). Visitors connect, mostly over BLE, and play with the effects. They cannot reconfigure the fixture, and nothing they do survives a reboot or an idle timeout.

Firmware: firmware/player_app/kiosk_ffi.rs (state, gate, protocol) and main.cpp (persistence, revert, pushes, console). Protocol: shared/protocol/proto/ledmapper.proto (“Kiosk mode”). HITL gate: //pi/hitl/harness:kiosk_netstack.

  • Capability: welcome.features contains "kiosk". The app sends get_kiosk_status after hello.

  • Refusals: Error{code: "kiosk_locked"}.

  • Errors from set_kiosk_config:

    • kiosk_secret_required: enabling with no secret stored or given.

    • kiosk_no_effect: capturing with no deck-A effect loaded.

    • kiosk_bad_config: a secret that is empty or longer than 32 bytes.

The gate¶

lm_kiosk_gate(src, frame) runs in main.cpp ahead of every handler:

  • lm_ws_dispatch, which serves wss and the BLE player transport, before the live mailbox;

  • ws_compute_reply;

  • the old vendor wss handler.

So it also covers OTA and sharded uploads. The source (SRC_WSS / SRC_BLE) selects the unlock that applies.

While the kiosk is enabled and the source is not owner-unlocked, each ClientMessage arm is classified (kiosk_ffi::classify):

  • Play (allowed; counts as activity): set_playback, submit_effect (either deck), set_effect, set_uniforms (the live channel too), set_texture, set_brightness (no cap), set_crossfade, blend_keypoints, and an upload_chunk of kind EFFECT.

  • Read (allowed; not activity): hello, time_sync_ping, the get/list queries (including get_stored_map, the map download), set_perf / get_perf_report, set_time_base, get_ota_status, get_kiosk_status, kiosk_unlock.

  • Refuse: everything else:

    • mapping, map/topology/pose uploads, LED count;

    • rename, hardware/colour config, set_jit, set_fps;

    • keypoint save/delete, sensors/drivers;

    • OTA writes, the FX debugger, Wi-Fi policy;

    • set_kiosk_config;

    • any arm not yet classified. A new arm is refused until it is classified.

Improv Wi-Fi provisioning is refused while the kiosk is enabled: improv_ble_take_credentials drops the credentials and fails the RPC. Owner unlock does not re-enable it; disable the kiosk first.

Persistence¶

  • Nothing a locked message does is persisted. persist_if_upload checks lm_kiosk_msg_locked(), so there are no /lfs/fx.pb / fx_sel.pb / pose writes.

  • An owner-unlocked connection behaves normally and persists.

  • Files. All are written with the atomic fs_write_file (tmp → fsync → rename). The snapshot frames are written before the record, so a power cut never leaves a record pointing at a torn snapshot.

    • /lfs/kiosk.cfg: the 112-byte record. It holds enabled, has_attract, the timeout, the attract brightness, the secret and the attract effect id.

    • /lfs/kiosk_fx.pb: the attract effect, as a submit_effect frame re-encoded from deck A, with JIT patches undone.

    • /lfs/kiosk_un.pb: its live uniforms, as a set_uniforms frame.

  • Boot. With the kiosk enabled and a snapshot stored, the device replays the two snapshot frames instead of fx.pb / fx_sel.pb. It then parks deck B, sets the crossfade to deck A and restores the attract brightness.

Idle revert¶

  • Every Play op restarts the idle timer. An owner-unlocked connection’s Play ops count too: what shows is no longer the captured snapshot, so it reverts after the owner leaves unless they re-capture.

  • After idle_timeout_s (default 120, range 10..86400) with no visitor activity, lm_kiosk_tick asks for a revert. This applies whether or not anyone is connected, and only if a visitor changed something since the last revert.

  • The revert is the same replay as at boot, under the player mutex. Then the device pushes kiosk_status{event: REVERTED}.

  • While any connection is owner-unlocked, the timer is held.

  • Pushes, to every open connection, wss and the BLE central, each with its own owner_unlocked:

    • REVERTED;

    • ACTIVITY: the first visitor activity after the attract show;

    • CONFIG: owner config, or the console kiosk off.

  • The app counts down locally from idle_remaining_s and resets to idle_timeout_s whenever it sends a Play op.

  • There is no maximum session length.

Owner¶

  • Unlock: kiosk_unlock{secret} unlocks only the connection that sent it, until that connection drops. lm_kiosk_tick clears an unlock on any connect or disconnect edge of that transport.

  • Wrong secret: a 2 s lockout on further attempts.

  • The secret travels in the clear over BLE and is stored in plain text on flash. It keeps visitors out; it does not resist an attacker with the board.

  • Recovery: the USB serial console kiosk off disables the kiosk (persisted) and drops all unlocks. kiosk status (or bare kiosk) prints the state.

Enabling on a fixture¶

  1. Connect while the kiosk is disabled.

  2. Deploy the attract effect on deck A, tune its sliders, and set the brightness.

  3. Send set_kiosk_config{enabled: true, owner_secret, idle_timeout_s: 120}.

    • With no snapshot stored, enabling captures one.

    • Add capture_attract_from_current: true to re-capture.

  4. To change it later:

    1. kiosk_unlock{secret};

    2. deploy and tune;

    3. set_kiosk_config{capture_attract_from_current: true}.

Notes¶

  • BLE and Wi-Fi. On the netstack build, a wss client connecting kicks the BLE central (netstack_transport.cpp: the vestigial post-provision BLE link is dropped on the rising edge of a wss session). That doesn’t matter at a no-Wi-Fi site, but on a bench with Wi-Fi a BLE visitor is disconnected when a wss client connects.

  • BLE frame sizes. BLE frames are capped at 1536 B and replies are serialized. kiosk_status is under 120 B.
