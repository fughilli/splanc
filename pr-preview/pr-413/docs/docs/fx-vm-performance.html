FX-VM performance¶

Measured FX-VM performance across hardware platforms, fitted from the golden HITL benchmark bundles (web/tests/testdata/device-bench-*.json) captured on real devices over the rig. This file is auto-generated and pinned by CI — regenerate it with:

bazel run //web:gen_fx_vm_perf_doc

The numbers come from the same cost model the app and CI use: buildDeviceProfile (web/src/effects/deviceProfile.ts) fits the per-opcode table and validates it against held-out programs (profileValidation.ts), gated at the same tolerance as web/tests/deviceProfileHardware.test.ts. See docs/design/perf-monitoring.md for the methodology and EFFECTS.md for the narrative.

Builtin costs (effects-AI ranking)¶

Rendered verbatim from builtinCostsToPrompt — the per-builtin ranking the in-editor AI is fed, relative to a float multiply (each runs once per LED in shade()):

BUILTIN COSTS on this device (esp32c6 @ 160 MHz, measured on your board) — per call, relative to a float multiply. shade() runs each ONCE PER LED, so favor the cheap ones:
  abs 0.4x, sign 0.6x, floor 0.7x, min 0.9x, max 0.9x, step 0.9x, add 1.0x, hash 1.0x, mul 1.0x, sub 1.0x, clamp 1.0x, ceil 1.1x, dot 1.1x, fract 1.1x, mix 2.2x, div 2.4x, length 2.9x, mod 3.4x, distance 3.4x, sin 3.4x, palette0 3.8x, cos 4.1x, normalize 4.7x, cross 5.2x, exp 5.3x, smoothstep 5.3x, sqrt 5.7x, log 7.2x, hsv2rgb 7.8x, atan2 8.8x, tan 8.9x, pow 14x
Hoist the pricey builtins (pow/atan2/log/exp/length/normalize/distance/smoothstep) out of shade() into update() or precompute into state; do per-LED maths in fixed (fixed() around every literal; sin/cos of a fixed phase in turns is an integer LUT) or step/mix/polynomials; avoid length/normalize where a squared distance works (hidden sqrt). Every op also pays ~70 cycles of dispatch, so fewer ops is faster.

esp32c6 — esp32c6 (JIT)¶

The on-device RV32IM JIT is enabled (the shipped default). It compiles hot straight-line integer / fixed-point blocks to native RV32; float-heavy and control-flow-heavy code stays interpreted. Same calibration corpus and firmware as the interpreter section above — only set_jit differs — so compare per-opcode and per-program costs directly against it to see where the JIT pays off.

Builtin costs (effects-AI ranking)¶

Rendered verbatim from builtinCostsToPrompt — the per-builtin ranking the in-editor AI is fed, relative to a float multiply (each runs once per LED in shade()):

BUILTIN COSTS on this device (esp32c6 @ 160 MHz, measured on your board) — per call, relative to a float multiply. shade() runs each ONCE PER LED, so favor the cheap ones:
  abs 0.4x, sign 0.6x, floor 0.7x, step 0.7x, clamp 0.7x, min 0.9x, max 0.9x, add 1.0x, hash 1.0x, mul 1.0x, sub 1.0x, ceil 1.1x, dot 1.1x, fract 1.1x, mix 2.2x, div 2.4x, length 2.9x, mod 3.4x, distance 3.4x, sin 3.4x, palette0 3.8x, cos 4.1x, normalize 4.7x, cross 5.2x, smoothstep 5.2x, exp 5.3x, sqrt 5.7x, log 7.2x, hsv2rgb 7.9x, atan2 8.8x, tan 9.0x, pow 14x
Hoist the pricey builtins (pow/atan2/log/exp/length/normalize/distance/smoothstep) out of shade() into update() or precompute into state; do per-LED maths in fixed (fixed() around every literal; sin/cos of a fixed phase in turns is an integer LUT) or step/mix/polynomials; avoid length/normalize where a squared distance works (hidden sqrt). Every op also pays ~70 cycles of dispatch, so fewer ops is faster.
