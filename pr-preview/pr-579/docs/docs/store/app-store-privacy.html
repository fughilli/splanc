App Store Connect ▸ App Privacy answers¶

These are the answers to enter in App Store Connect ▸ Splanc ▸ App Privacy for the iOS app (dev.splanc.app). They match the privacy notice and replace the device-feedback-only table in docs/store-privacy-labels.md (#574 branch).

Ship gate. Enter a row only once the build that collects it is in review:

Page 1: data collection¶

  • Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? Yes.

  • Privacy Policy URL: the published privacy notice (https://github.com/fughilli/splanc/blob/main/docs/privacy.md today; see the owner decision on publishing it at https://splanc.io/privacy).

Page 2: data types¶

Apple asks, for each data type: is it linked to the user’s identity, is it used for tracking, and for which purposes. “Linked” is answered once per data type, so a type that is linked anywhere is answered Yes.

Tracking: No for every type. There is no advertising, no data broker and no cross-app tracking, so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Notes on the choices:

  • “App Functionality” in Apple’s definition includes preventing fraud, security, customer support and fixing crashes and performance, which covers the 18+ gate, caps and abuse suspension, problem reports and device feedback.

  • Analytics is chosen for device feedback because it measures how the app and players behave across installs.

  • Business monitoring (#559) adds no row. Its events have no identifiers and no user content (agent/relay/src/ops.ts, OPS_SCHEMA), so they are aggregate counts derived from the rows above.

  • The 18+ confirmation and the refund-information consent are records on the credits account, not separate data types.

Not collected¶

Answer not collected for:

  • Financial Info (payment info, credit info, other financial info): Apple handles In-App Purchase payment, and the iOS app never offers the Stripe web checkout (mayShowWebCheckout()).

  • Contact Info other than email (name, phone, physical address), Health & Fitness, Location (precise or coarse), Sensitive Info, Contacts, Browsing History, Search History.

  • User Content ▸ Emails or Text Messages, Photos or Videos, Audio Data, Gameplay Content. Camera frames for mapping are processed on the device. Voice in Acid Mode is recognised by Apple’s speech framework; Splanc only receives the text, as part of a prompt (see the owner decision on audio).

  • Usage Data ▸ Advertising Data, Other Usage Data.

  • Other Data.

Data not counted as collected¶

  • Sign-in name and email. The app asks Apple for no name or email scope, and Google only for openid. Google’s ID token still carries email, name and picture claims; the relay verifies the token and drops it without reading or keeping them (#583 signin.ts), so Email Address and Name are not added for sign-in. Email Address stays declared for the recovery email only.

  • The Free AI option and your own key send prompts straight from the device to OpenRouter or the user’s chosen provider, under the user’s own account. Neither we nor a partner of ours receive them.

  • Rate-limit IP counters (deleted within a day) and transient request handling are not used to locate or identify the user.

  • The cloud relay passes bridge commands through without storing them.

App Review notes¶

  • 5.1.2(i), third-party AI: credits requests go to Anthropic. The model setup sheet says so (“Requests go through Splanc’s server to Anthropic (Claude)”); see the owner decision on an explicit consent step.

  • 5.1.1(v), account deletion: Settings ▸ AI ▸ Delete account, with the forfeit warning.

  • Privacy policy link in the app: Settings ▸ Privacy (#574); see the owner decision on adding it to the credits flow and About.
