Google Play Console ▸ Data safety answers¶

There is no Play app today. Android users run the web app in Chrome, where no store form applies (docs/design/ai-billing.md › Store rules, owner decision 1: no Play-distributed app for now). This form is prepared for the day a Play build (for example a Trusted Web Activity) ships. It matches the privacy notice and replaces the device-feedback-only section of docs/store-privacy-labels.md (#574 branch).

Before using it, read the payments note at the end: a Play build can’t use the Stripe web checkout for credits as it stands.

Overview questions¶

Question

Answer

Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?

Yes

Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?

Yes (HTTPS / WSS to our relay; the LAN link to players is local and isn’t collection)

Which of the following methods of account creation does your app support?

Accounts are created anonymously (no username or password) for Splanc AI credits, optionally with a verified email for recovery, and optionally linked to Sign in with Google / Apple (#583). Select “OAuth” and “Other: anonymous account”

Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?

Yes: in the app (Settings ▸ AI ▸ Delete account; Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Delete my data)

Delete account URL (required for apps that create accounts)

https://splanc.io/app/#/settings/ai (the same web app has Delete account), or the privacy notice’s “Your rights” section. See the owner decision

Has your app been independently validated against a global security standard?

No

Is your app’s data collection committed to the Play Families policy?

No (not directed at children)

“Shared” below means transferred to a third party. Play doesn’t count transfers to service providers processing for us (Cloudflare, Anthropic, Stripe, Resend, Grafana Labs), or transfers the user starts and expects (their own OpenRouter or provider key), as sharing. So Shared is No throughout.

Sign-in note¶

Google’s ID token carries the user’s email, name and picture. The relay verifies it and drops it without reading or keeping those claims (#583 signin.ts). Play counts data the app’s server receives as collected, even briefly, so declare:

  • Personal info ▸ Name: collected, not shared, processed ephemerally, optional, Account management (only when the user links Google);

  • Personal info ▸ Email address: keep the row above (not ephemeral, because the recovery email’s hash is kept). Sign-in adds nothing to it.

Apple sends no name or email, because the app asks for no scopes.

Not collected¶

  • Location (approximate or precise).

  • Personal info other than email and name (see the sign-in note): address, phone, race and ethnicity, political or religious beliefs, sexual orientation, other info.

  • Financial info other than purchase history.

  • Health and fitness, Messages (emails, SMS), Photos and videos, Audio (voice in Acid Mode is recognised by the device’s speech service; only the text reaches a prompt), Files and docs, Calendar, Contacts, Web browsing.

  • App activity ▸ in-app search history, installed apps, other actions.

Payments note (blocking for a Play build)¶

The web and Android channel buys credits through Stripe Checkout. Play’s payments policy requires Google Play Billing for digital goods in a Play app, except where alternative billing is allowed (for example the US, with fees) (ai-billing.md › Store rules). A Play build therefore needs either Play Billing (a new purchase channel, which would then add Financial info ▸ Purchase history from Google instead of Stripe) or an enrolled alternative billing program. This is an owner decision before any Play submission.
