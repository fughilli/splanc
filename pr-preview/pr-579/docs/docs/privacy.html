Splanc privacy notice¶

Last updated: 2026-11-20.

This notice covers:

  • the Splanc app on the web (splanc.io/app), on iOS and on Android (the web app in Chrome);

  • Splanc players (the firmware on your lights);

  • the Splanc cloud services: the relay that runs Splanc AI credits, problem reports and remote control, and the splanc.io website.

[OWNER: legal name] Splanc is made by Fug Studio, a trade name of [OWNER: the LLC’s legal name and registered address], which is the controller of the data described here (“we”, “us”). Privacy contact: [OWNER: privacy contact email].

The short version¶

  • Splanc runs on your own network. Your maps, effects, device names and Wi-Fi details stay on your devices unless you choose to send something.

  • Nothing is collected unless you use a feature that needs it. Each one is listed below, with what it sends, why, how long we keep it and who helps us process it.

  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across apps or websites. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

  • Splanc AI credits don’t store your prompts. They pass through our server to Anthropic, which keeps them for up to 30 days.

  • You can delete your data in the app: your AI credits account under Settings ▸ AI, and device feedback under Settings ▸ Privacy.

What stays on your device¶

These are stored only in the app on your phone or computer, or on your players, and never sent to us:

  • fixture maps, effects and saved looks (keypoints);

  • device names, rooms and poses;

  • Wi-Fi names and passwords (they go from the app to your player over Bluetooth or your local network, never to us);

  • camera images used for mapping (processed in the app);

  • AI chat history;

  • API keys you enter for your own AI provider.

Splanc AI credits¶

Splanc AI credits let you pay for Claude in the app without your own API key.

What we keep:

  • An anonymous account. The app creates a random account id (acct_…) and a sign-in token, which it stores on your device. The account holds no name, no email (unless you add a recovery email) and no device name.

  • Your 18+ confirmation. Buying credits is for adults only. We record that you confirmed you are 18 or older, and when.

  • Your ledger: each purchase, refund and AI request, as numbers: credits, token counts, the model, the time and an opaque request id. It never holds your prompts or the AI’s replies.

  • Purchases: the store’s transaction id, the pack, and whether it was bought in the iOS app or on the web. We never keep your card or other payment details, and we don’t keep Apple’s receipt after we have checked it.

  • On iOS, your refund-information choice. If you say yes (it is off by default, and you can change it in AI settings), when you ask Apple for a refund we tell Apple how much of that credit pack you used, as Apple’s refund process asks. Without your yes, we tell Apple nothing.

Payments:

  • iOS app: you pay Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; we receive a signed record of the transaction.

  • Web and Android: you pay on Stripe’s secure checkout page (card, Apple Pay or Google Pay). Stripe collects your payment details, email and the billing address it needs to work out sales tax (Stripe Tax). We receive the payment’s status, the amount and pack, and any refund or dispute, linked to your account id. Stripe’s own privacy policy covers what you enter on its page.

Why: to provide the credits you bought, keep your balance correct, prevent fraud and abuse (daily and monthly caps, suspending an account that breaks the rules), handle refunds, and keep the financial records the law requires.

Deleting your account (Settings ▸ AI ▸ Delete account):

  • Any credits left are forfeited and not refunded. The app warns you before you confirm.

  • We first tell Apple to end any Sign in with Apple link (we revoke Apple’s token for Splanc and erase it). Then the account is closed, your recovery email is erased, every Apple or Google sign-in is removed, and every device is signed out.

  • The ledger and purchase records, which hold no name or contact details, are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires [OWNER: retention period, e.g. 7 years].

  • Apple’s and Stripe’s own refund processes stay available to you.

Optional recovery email¶

An anonymous account lives only in the app’s storage. If you clear the app’s data or change phones, you lose the sign-in token and your credits with it. An email lets you get the account back. It is optional: you can buy credits without one, after the app tells you that the credits can’t then be recovered.

  • What we keep: a scrambled copy of the address (a keyed hash), which is all we need to find your account when you ask to recover it, and a masked hint such as k•••@gmail.com (the first letter and the domain) so the app can show which email you used. We don’t keep the address itself.

  • Codes: to check the address is yours, and to recover the account, we send a 6-digit code. It is valid for 10 minutes and works once. We keep only a scrambled copy of the code until it is used or expires.

  • Who sends it: our email provider, Resend, receives the address and the message only to deliver the code.

  • What it’s used for: only verifying the address and recovering your account. We can’t send you anything else, because we don’t have your address.

  • Remove it in Settings ▸ AI at any time. Deleting the account erases it.

  • Recovering your account signs it out on your other devices.

Sign in with Apple or Google¶

You can link your Splanc AI credits account to Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, so that your credits follow you to another device. It is optional.

What we keep, for each sign-in you link (at most one Apple and one Google):

  • The anonymous id Apple or Google gives Splanc for you. It is not your name or email. We use it only to find your account when you sign in on another device.

  • Which Splanc app it came from (the iOS app or the web app), because Apple needs that to end the sign-in later.

  • When you linked it, which Settings ▸ AI shows.

  • For Sign in with Apple only, Apple’s token for Splanc, encrypted. We keep it only so we can tell Apple to end the sign-in when you remove it or delete your account. We never use it to get data from Apple.

To stop a sign-in being replayed, we also keep a random one-time code for 10 minutes. It isn’t linked to you.

What we don’t keep:

  • your name, email, a hashed email or your profile photo. We ask Apple for no name or email, and Google only for the basic sign-in. Google’s sign-in answer still contains your name, email and picture; our server checks the sign-in and discards the answer without reading or keeping them;

  • any Google token;

  • the sign-in id in our logs. Our logs record that a sign-in happened, with our own account id, for 30 days.

Who is involved: you sign in on Apple’s or Google’s own screen. Our server asks Apple and Google to confirm the sign-in. In the web app, Apple’s and Google’s sign-in scripts load only when you open the sign-in screen. Apple tells us if you stop using Sign in with Apple with Splanc or delete your Apple ID, and we then remove the link.

Your controls (Settings ▸ AI):

  • Sign out forgets the sign-in on this device only. Nothing is deleted on our server.

  • Remove a sign-in deletes what we keep for it. For Apple, we also revoke and erase Apple’s token.

  • Delete account does the same for every sign-in, then closes the account (see Deleting your account).

  • If a sign-in already belongs to another Splanc AI account, nothing is merged: you choose which account this device uses.

AI problem reports¶

Every AI reply has a Report button. A report helps us find and fix bugs in the AI.

  • You see exactly what is sent before you send it, section by section.

  • Your conversation and the effect code are off by default. You can switch them on, and switch any other section off.

  • Always included: the reasons you pick, your note, the app version, a coarse browser label (for example “Chrome 131 on Android”), the AI model, and what the app noticed went wrong.

  • Never included: API keys and tokens, Wi-Fi names and passwords, device names, IP and MAC addresses, email addresses, images and where you are. The app masks these, and our server scrubs every report again.

  • Anonymous reports are allowed. To stop floods we keep a salted hash of the sender’s IP address (or of the paired bridge), never the IP itself.

  • Where it goes: a private store on our relay (Cloudflare). It is never posted publicly. If a group of reports turns out to be a real bug, we write our own public issue without your content.

  • Kept 90 days, then deleted automatically. To delete one sooner, ask us (the app keeps a receipt for each report it sent, which identifies it).

Bug reports¶

When the app or an AI agent offers to file a bug and you confirm, the text you approve becomes a public issue on the project’s GitHub page. It includes the app and firmware versions and board details, never a player’s name or address, your effects or your saved looks. It is public as long as the issue exists.

Device feedback (“Send feedback to Fug Studio”)¶

Off by default. If you turn it on in Settings ▸ Privacy, you choose any of four categories:

  • Performance: frame rates (the target, the achieved rate, and whether an effect is lagging), free memory, and the number of LEDs.

  • Reliability:

    • uptime;

    • why a player last restarted (power on, update, watchdog, crash, brownout) and how many times since power-on;

    • Wi-Fi joins, lost connections and signal strength;

    • Bluetooth connections that dropped.

  • Mesh: how a mesh of players delivers updates: mode, size, delivery rate, airtime, relay load and mode switches.

  • Usage:

    • how many players the app knows;

    • how often you open the app and which main screens you use;

    • how many effects you send to players.

    • Never which effects you send, what they are, or what you make.

Each upload also includes:

  • the app version and the platform (web, iOS or Android);

  • for each player, its firmware version, its board type and how it is connected;

  • random codes for the app and for each player.

What we never collect this way:

  • names or device names, Wi-Fi network names or passwords;

  • IP or MAC addresses, or location;

  • maps, effects, chats, or anything you type.

The codes are pseudonyms.

  • They are random, derived from a secret that only your app holds.

  • They change every 30 days.

  • They are never linked to your Splanc AI account, a bridge, or your IP address. Your IP address is used only to rate-limit uploads, and it is not stored with the data.

See it yourself. Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ See exactly what’s sent shows the exact text of the next upload, and of the last one.

Why: to find bugs, crashes and performance problems, and to tune features such as the mesh, with your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by turning the setting off.

Where it goes: to our relay (Cloudflare), then to our private monitoring workspace (Grafana Cloud). Only Fug Studio staff can see it.

How long: at most 90 days, then it is deleted automatically.

Delete it: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Delete my data.

  • It deletes everything sent under every code your app used, and turns sending off.

  • It finishes within 7 days.

  • If you turn sending back on, it starts with new codes.

Cloud relay (remote control)¶

If you pair a bridge so that an AI assistant (such as a ChatGPT or Claude connector) can control your lights, the relay passes its requests to your bridge and the answers back. Those requests can include effect code, the shape of your fixtures, player names and their local addresses. The relay passes them through and does not store them. It stores no passwords, Wi-Fi details or device keys, and keeps only hashes of access tokens. You can run your own relay instead (see the cloud agent guide).

Running the service¶

  • Business monitoring. Our servers send counts and labels to a private Grafana Cloud workspace: for example, how many credits were bought or spent, Anthropic costs, error rates and how many problem reports came in. These events go through an allowlist that keeps only numbers, yes/no values and short labels. They never contain account ids, request or transaction ids, IP addresses, emails, names, card data, prompts, replies or effect code.

  • Request logs. Our relay’s logs (Cloudflare, and the billing events in Grafana Cloud) hold ids, token counts, status codes and errors, never prompt content. They are kept for 30 days.

  • Rate limits. To stop abuse, we count requests per IP address (for example, how many accounts or email codes an address asked for). These counters are deleted when their window ends: within an hour, or at most a day.

  • Payment webhooks. We remember the id of each Stripe event for 30 days so that a repeat isn’t processed twice.

  • The splanc.io website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

Why we may use it (legal bases)¶

If you are in the UK, the EU or a similar jurisdiction, these are our legal bases:

  • To provide a service you asked for (contract): your Splanc AI credits account, sign-ins you link, purchases, AI requests through credits, the cloud relay, and bug reports you choose to file.

  • Your consent: device feedback, AI problem reports, the recovery email, and the iOS refund-information choice. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.

  • Legal obligations: keeping financial and tax records.

  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and working (rate limits, abuse prevention, request logs) and understanding the business in aggregate (counts without identifiers).

Your rights and choices¶

  • Delete:

    • your AI credits account: Settings ▸ AI ▸ Delete account;

    • your device feedback: Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Delete my data;

    • an AI problem report: ask us;

    • your recovery email: Settings ▸ AI;

    • an Apple or Google sign-in: Settings ▸ AI ▸ Remove.

  • Stop: turn device feedback off, choose not to send reports, and use the AI options that keep prompts on your device or with your own provider.

  • See: Settings ▸ AI shows your balance and recent activity; Settings ▸ Privacy shows exactly what device feedback sends.

  • Ask us: for a copy of your data, a correction, deletion, or any question, contact [OWNER: privacy contact email]. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need you to prove the account is yours from the app (for example, with your account’s sign-in or a code sent to your recovery email).

  • Complain: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children¶

Splanc is not directed at children. Buying Splanc AI credits is for people 18 or older, and the app asks you to confirm that before your first purchase. The other features don’t ask for your age and don’t collect personal information about anyone.

Changes¶

We will update this notice before a change in what we collect takes effect, and change the date at the top.
