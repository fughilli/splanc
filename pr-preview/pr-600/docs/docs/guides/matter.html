Splanc lamps in Apple Home and other Matter apps¶

The Splanc bridge includes a Matter bridge. Matter is the smart-home standard that Apple Home, Home Assistant, Amazon Alexa, Samsung SmartThings and Google Home all use. You add the bridge to your smart-home app once. Every Splanc lamp the bridge finds then appears as a dimmable light, including lamps you add later.

Apple Home / Home Assistant / Alexa / SmartThings
        │  Matter, on your home network (no cloud)
        ▼
  Splanc bridge (Pi, or any always-on computer)
        │  the Splanc app protocol
        ▼
  your Splanc lamps

What each lamp can do in your smart-home app:

In the app

On the lamp

On / off

The lamp turns on or off, with the ON/OFF transition you set in the Splanc app.

Brightness

The lamp’s brightness (the same master dimmer as in the Splanc app).

Its state

A tap on the lamp’s lid, or a change in the Splanc app, shows up in the smart-home app.

Its name

The lamp’s name in the Splanc app, with its room: Coil (Bedroom).

Unreachable

Shown when the bridge can’t reach the lamp (it is unplugged, or off your Wi-Fi).

Effects, colours and saved looks stay in the Splanc app: Matter has no way to describe a Splanc effect. Colour control from the smart-home app is planned. If you use Home Assistant, the Splanc integration for Home Assistant also lets you pick saved looks.

On/off needs lamp firmware with the power feature (the touch lid and the ON/OFF transitions). On older firmware, “off” sets the brightness to 0 and “on” brings the last brightness back. Update the lamp from the Splanc app to get real on/off.

What works with which app¶

The Splanc bridge is not Matter-certified yet. It identifies itself with the Matter test vendor ID, so some apps warn before adding it:

App

Works?

Notes

Apple Home (iOS 18+)

Yes

iOS says Uncertified Accessory. Tap Add Anyway. Remote control and automations need a home hub (HomePod or Apple TV).

Home Assistant

Yes

Through its Matter integration (it adds a Matter server). Or use the Splanc integration instead.

Amazon Alexa

Not tested by us yet

Alexa is reported to accept uncertified devices. Needs an Echo that is a Matter controller.

Samsung SmartThings

Not tested by us yet

Needs a SmartThings hub or a Matter-capable SmartThings device.

Google Home

No (for now)

Google only lets certified Matter devices into normal accounts. Use Home Assistant’s Google Assistant link meanwhile (see below).

We are working on certification. Until it is done, Splanc does not claim “Works with” any of these platforms.

What you need¶

  • A Splanc bridge on the same network as your lamps: the Splanc Bridge box, a Raspberry Pi you set up yourself (Set up a Splanc bridge on a Raspberry Pi), or any always-on computer (see Build your own bridge).

  • Your smart-home app’s hub where it needs one (a HomePod or Apple TV for Apple Home away from home; an Echo for Alexa; a SmartThings hub).

  • IPv6 turned on in your router. It is on by default almost everywhere; Matter needs it on your local network, not on the internet.

1. Find the bridge’s Matter code¶

The bridge has its own Matter pairing code. It is different from the cloud pairing code (the ABCD-EFGH one) and it doesn’t expire.

  • OLED screen: move the joystick to the Matter page (right after Scan to pair). It shows a QR code and the 11-digit code, like 3497-011-2332.

  • HDMI screen: while no cloud pairing code is up, the page shows Add to your smart home (Matter) with the code and the QR code.

  • Over SSH: splanc-bridge matter-pair prints the code.

2. Add the bridge in your app¶

Apple Home: tap + ▸ Add Accessory, and scan the QR code on the bridge’s screen (or tap More options and type the 11 digits). When iOS says Uncertified Accessory, tap Add Anyway. Pick a room for the bridge. Each lamp then shows up as a light; give each one its own room in the Home app.

Home Assistant: go to Settings ▸ Devices & services ▸ Add integration ▸ Matter. Home Assistant installs its Matter server the first time. Then add the bridge from the Home Assistant Companion app on your phone (Settings ▸ Devices & services ▸ Devices ▸ Add device ▸ Add Matter device) and scan the QR code. The lamps appear as lights under one Splanc Bridge device.

Amazon Alexa: in the Alexa app, tap Devices ▸ + ▸ Add Device ▸ Other ▸ Matter, then scan the QR code or type the code.

SmartThings: tap + ▸ Add device ▸ Scan QR code.

Once the bridge is added, the OLED’s Matter page says Added to and the app’s name, and the code is hidden.

3. Add it to a second app¶

Matter lets several apps control the same lamps (for example Apple Home and Home Assistant). There are two ways to add the bridge to another app:

  • In the first app, use its share or pairing mode option (Apple Home: the bridge’s settings ▸ Turn On Pairing Mode). It gives you a new code for the other app.

  • On the bridge, go to the OLED’s Matter page and press the joystick, or run splanc-bridge matter-pair over SSH. The bridge shows its code again for 15 minutes.

One controller at a time¶

A Splanc lamp talks to one app at a time. When you open the Splanc app and use a lamp, the app takes over, and you can work on effects and maps without any lag. Meanwhile:

  • turning the lamp on or off from your smart-home app still works (the bridge takes the lamp back when the Splanc app has been idle for 15 seconds);

  • the smart-home app may show the lamp’s old state for a few minutes. The bridge checks the lamp again within 1 to 10 minutes, and at once when you control the lamp from the smart-home app.

Use one smart-home path per lamp: either the Matter bridge, or the Home Assistant integration talking to the lamp directly, not both.

Remove the bridge from your apps¶

Remove it in each app as usual (Apple Home: the bridge’s settings ▸ Remove Bridge). To make the bridge forget every app at once, run splanc-bridge matter-reset --yes over SSH. The lamps and the cloud pairing are not affected, and the bridge shows a code again.

To turn the Matter bridge off, put SPLANC_MATTER=0 in splanc-bridge.conf on the card’s boot partition (Changing settings later).

Google Home¶

Google Home accepts only Matter devices that the Connectivity Standards Alliance has certified. Until the Splanc bridge is certified:

  • With Home Assistant: add your lamps to Home Assistant (with the Matter bridge or the Splanc integration), then expose them to Google with Home Assistant’s Google Assistant integration (Home Assistant Cloud, or the manual setup).

  • Developers only: a Google Home Developer Console project with a Matter integration for vendor ID 0xFFF1 and product ID 0x8001 lets the members of that project add the bridge to their own home. This is a developer tool, not a way to share the lamps with your household.

Build your own bridge¶

The Matter bridge is part of the free, open Splanc bridge software. You don’t need to buy the Splanc Bridge box.

On a Raspberry Pi (Pi 5, 4, 3 B/B+/A+, or Zero 2 W): follow Set up a Splanc bridge on a Raspberry Pi. The Matter bridge is on by default. An OLED HAT is optional but handy, because it shows the Matter code; without one, use the HDMI page or splanc-bridge matter-pair over SSH. The Splanc Bridge box is a Pi 3 A+ with the Waveshare 1.3” OLED HAT in a case: the same image, the same software.

On Raspberry Pi OS or any Linux computer: install the bridge service with curl -fsSL https://splanc.io/bridge/install.sh | sudo sh (see the Pi guide). The Matter bridge starts with it.

On any always-on computer (Linux, macOS, Windows, Node.js 20 or newer):

npx -y https://splanc.io/agent/splanc-agent.tgz bridge
# in another terminal:
npx -y https://splanc.io/agent/splanc-agent.tgz bridge-ctl status       # "matter": the code + QR payload
npx -y https://splanc.io/agent/splanc-agent.tgz bridge-ctl matter-pair  # pairing again, for another app

Things to check on a computer you set up yourself:

  • It is on the same network (the same subnet, no client isolation) as the lamps and the smart-home hub.

  • The firewall lets in UDP 5540 (Matter) and UDP 5353 (mDNS). Use another port with --matter-port <port> if 5540 is taken.

  • In Docker, use host networking (--network host): Matter’s discovery doesn’t cross a Docker bridge network.

  • The bridge keeps its Matter identity and the apps it was added to under matter/ in its state directory (--state-dir; by default ~/.local/state/splanc, or $SPLANC_BRIDGE_STATE_DIR). Back it up, or you will need to add the bridge to your apps again.

Troubleshooting¶

What you see

What to do

The app can’t find the bridge after scanning the code

Make sure the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the bridge (not a guest network) and IPv6 is on in your router. A Pi 3 or Zero 2 W only sees 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

The Matter page says Matter is off

SPLANC_MATTER=0 is set in splanc-bridge.conf: remove the line.

The Matter page shows an error

Usually another program uses UDP port 5540. splanc-bridge logs shows the details.

A lamp is Not Responding / unreachable

The bridge can’t reach the lamp: check that it is powered and on the Wi-Fi (the bridge’s Fixtures page shows a red dot for it).

A lamp shows the wrong on/off state for a while

Someone is using it in the Splanc app (see One controller at a time). It catches up within minutes.

A new lamp doesn’t show up

The bridge looks for new lamps every 5 minutes. Make sure the lamp is on the same network.

Google Home refuses the bridge

Expected until the bridge is certified: see Google Home.
